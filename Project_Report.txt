STUDENT PERFORMANCE & ACADEMIC ANALYTICS
DATA ANALYTICS PROJECT REPORT

1. INTRODUCTION
This project analyzes a synthetic student-performance dataset to understand academic outcomes and identify factors associated with final scores.

2. OBJECTIVES
- Analyze student academic performance.
- Compare subjects, schools and classes.
- Compare different study methods.
- Examine relationships between study habits, attendance and final scores.
- Create visualizations and recommendations.

3. DATASET
The dataset contains 1,200 student-subject records with study hours, attendance, assignment completion, sleep hours, previous score, final score, grade, study method and internet access.

4. TOOLS
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib and Excel.

5. KEY KPIs
Total records: 1,200
Unique students: 1,200
Average final score: 59.33
Average attendance: 81.94%
Average study hours/day: 2.79

6. KEY FINDINGS
Highest-performing subject: Mathematics.
Highest-performing school: Ryan International.
Highest-performing study method: Coaching.
Study hours, attendance, assignment completion and previous score can be analyzed as factors associated with final scores; correlation does not prove causation.

7. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Identify students with low attendance for early academic support.
- Encourage consistent study routines.
- Use assignment completion as an early-warning indicator.
- Compare study methods across student groups before recommending one method.
- Build a dashboard with filters for class, subject and school.

8. CONCLUSION
The project demonstrates a complete analytics workflow: data preparation, KPI calculation, aggregation, visualization, relationship analysis and business-style recommendations.

NOTE: All data is synthetic and created for educational purposes.
